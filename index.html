About Me

The Editor
Behind the Frame

"Every cut is a decision. Every frame is intentional. I don't just edit — I sculpt stories."

Hi, I'm Jeetu — a professional video editor based in New Delhi, India. I specialise in creating cinematic, high-impact content across a wide range of formats — from AI films and fiction to podcasts, music films and information reels.

With 5+ years of experience and 100+ projects under my belt, I understand what it takes to turn raw footage into something that genuinely moves an audience. Whether it's a 60-second reel or a full-length film, I bring the same dedication to every single frame.
